## Genres that work well as a first version

- **Dodge and collect** — move around, grab good things, avoid bad things, it speeds up.
- **Endless runner** — jump and duck over obstacles, the distance is the score.
- **Top-down arena** — waves of enemies come at you, shoot or bonk them.
- **Platformer** — jump between platforms to reach a goal, a few short levels.
- **Two-player duel** — WASD versus arrow keys: pong, tanks, sumo, tag.

## Ideas for later rounds

Boss fight · levels that get harder · high score saved on the laptop · combo multiplier ·
screen shake and particles · sound effects and music · secret cheat code · a drawing of yours as the hero
